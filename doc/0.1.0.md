# Kharchio 0.1.0 — Release Notes

Kharchio’s first release brings expense tracking, monthly budgets, spending analysis, and offline voice entry together in one Android app. Record purchases manually or describe them aloud, then see where your money goes. No account or cloud backend is required.

## Expense tracking

- Add multiple expenses in one session using individual cards for each purchase.
- Capture a title, optional description, category, amount, and expense date.
- Review and edit saved transactions, or delete them with confirmation.
- Keep recording, add-card, and save controls accessible while scrolling. The final card can scroll upward to leave room for its category dropdown.

## Dashboard and analysis

- View the current month’s spending, recent transactions, and a comparison with the previous month.
- Explore spending through a colourful donut chart with category amounts and percentage breakdowns. The chart collapses into a compact bar as you scroll.
- Search transactions and filter spending by category.
- Drill down from yearly analysis to monthly calendars and individual days to review your expenses.
- Select analysis years from 2026 through 2046.

## Monthly Budget Planner

- Set and edit a spending budget for each month and year.
- See Remaining Budget on the dashboard when a monthly budget exists; otherwise, continue seeing Total Spent.
- Review the set budget alongside savings or overspending in monthly and daily analysis.
- Delete a budget without deleting the month’s expenses. Months without a user-set budget stay out of the planner list.

## Offline voice expenses

- Record English or Hinglish purchases, with Automatic language selection and pause/resume controls.
- Use Whisper Small for transcription and Qwen3 for extracting expense titles, descriptions, categories, and amounts.
- Review the extracted purchases and turn them into editable expense cards before saving through the regular recording flow.
- Choose supported CPU, GPU, or Qualcomm NPU processing options according to your device and downloaded models.
- Download AI models separately from Settings, with progress, download speed, pause/resume, and file verification. Delete downloaded models whenever you need to reclaim storage.
- Model availability checks help prevent recording or processing with missing files. Manual expense entry works without downloading AI models.

## Android home-screen widgets

- **Quick Expense, 4×1:** shortcuts for adding an expense or opening quick voice capture. Quick voice capture saves extracted expenses automatically and offers Undo.
- **Add Expense, 2×1:** opens a compact entry popup above the home screen. Add saves your expense using the current date and time without navigating through the main app.
- **Spending Pie, 4×2:** displays current-month spending as a donut, with slice percentages, a central total, and a scrollable category list showing amounts.

## Personalisation and local storage

- Choose Light, Dark, or System appearance and INR or USD currency. Currency selection does not convert existing amounts.
- Customise your profile photo and display name, saved locally on your device.
- Create categories with coloured icons. Categories appear alphabetically, with Others always last, including in widgets.
- Keep expenses and settings on your device. Voice audio and temporary transcripts are not saved to the expense database.
- Use Danger Zone to reset expense records while keeping categories, preferences, and downloaded models.
