# Kharchio 1.0.0 Release Notes

Kharchio 1.0.0 can turn likely payment notifications into editable expense drafts on Android. Review each draft before saving it, or leave it in the pending list for later.

## Payment notifications

- Go to **Profile & Settings → Payment notifications** to grant notification access. Enable **Allow review alerts** if Android asks for permission to send Kharchio alerts while the app is in the background.
- Kharchio checks incoming notifications on the device for signs of a completed payment and prepares a draft with the amount, merchant, and a suggested category when it can identify them.
- Tap an individual Kharchio alert to open that draft. Use the notification group summary or **Review all** to open **Review detected expenses**. Android may expand a collapsed group first.
- Repeated alerts with the same payment amount and transaction timestamp are treated as one review. This can also combine separate payments for the same amount in the same second.

## Reviewing detected expenses

- Use the same **Expense** and **Refund** card as manual entry. Edit the title, description, category, and amount before saving. The review uses the current date and the currency detected in the notification.
- Tap the AI icon to refine the draft with your configured AI mode. On-device extraction uses Qwen. In online mode, tapping the icon sends a redacted copy of the message to your selected provider.
- Choose **Review later** to keep the draft in the pending list. Tap a row to reopen it, swipe left to dismiss it, or use **Clear all reviews** to dismiss every pending draft after confirming.

## Settings and layout

- Pending reviews, Categories, and Budget Planner keep their page controls in place while the lists scroll inside their cards.
- Settings rows have consistent spacing, and review, backup import, and model deletion confirmations use the app’s dialog styling.

## Upgrading from an earlier build

The app identifier changed from `com.expense.kharchio` to `com.somesh.kharchio`. Android treats 1.0.0 as a separate app from builds that use the old identifier. To move your saved expenses, refunds, and monthly limits, export a backup from the old app and import it in 1.0.0 under **Profile & Settings → Data Backup** before removing the old app. Re-enter API keys and download offline models again as needed.
